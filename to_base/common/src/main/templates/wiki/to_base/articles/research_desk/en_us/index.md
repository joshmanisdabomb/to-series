# Introduction
The {{"template": "introduction"}}. It offers an in-game interface for the online wiki for the {{"of": "to_base:tag / to_base:to_series"}} of mods.

# User Interface
To open the user interface, right-click the {{"of": "self"}}. If you're holding an item relevant to the mod, you are taken straight to the item's article. Otherwise, the main menu opens.

## Main Menu
From the homepage, you can open a list of all articles for the To mods. You can also view lists of just blocks, items, and mobs available in the mods, or lists of articles from individual To mods.

You can also click highlighted items in your inventory to view their articles.

The top of the homepage allows you to search for articles by name.

## Articles
Articles are clickable wiki-like pages that provide information about a given topic, such as a block or an item.

# Placement
When placed down in the world, the {{"of": "self"}} will face the player. It is 2 blocks wide; when the player clicks to place the desk, the left side of the block will be at the cursor.

If the right side of the desk is obstructed, the desk will be placed right side at the cursor instead.
