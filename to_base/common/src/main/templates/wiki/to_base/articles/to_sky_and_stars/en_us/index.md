# Plans
The main goal of this mod is to add a launch pad to the game, which can fire:
- Missiles to deploy explosives to locations in your world.
- Satellites to hover over your world and provide mapping or military support to locations.
- Probes to explore space and provide information on planets and solar systems to travel to.
- Asteroid miners to extract resources and return them to your world.
- Rockets to travel to other planets.

This mod aims to make the starting solar system curated, and then other solar systems further out can be randomly generated, similar to {{"of": "to_base:mod_version / minecraft:20w14infinity"}}.
