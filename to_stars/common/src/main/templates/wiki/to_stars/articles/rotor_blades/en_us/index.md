# Introduction

The {{"template": "introduction", "description": "a block"}}. Its primary use is to convert {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:heat"}} from {{"of": "minecraft:block / to_stars:boiler", "plural": true}} into {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_base:to_energy"}}.

It has a secondary use as a fan to blow entities when powered by {{"of": "to_base:concept / minecraft:redstone"}}.

# Mounting

{{"of": "self"}} need a sturdy block directly behind it to attach to, and cannot be placed directly beside another {{"of": "self"}} facing the same direction.

If the supporting block is removed, the {{"of": "self"}} breaks.

# Usage

## Turbine

When mounted to a {{"of": "to_base:tag / to_stars:turbine"}} and an active {{"of": "minecraft:block / to_stars:boiler"}} supplied with {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:heat"}} is directly beneath it within 3 blocks unobstructed, the {{"of": "self"}} start spinning.

Any {{"of": "self"}} mounted to a {{"of": "to_base:tag / to_stars:turbine"}} cannot be powered with {{"of": "to_base:concept / minecraft:redstone"}} or push entities. Only steam from the {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:heat"}} of a {{"of": "minecraft:block / to_stars:boiler"}} spins the blades.

The {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_base:to_energy"}} generated by the steam below is dependent on the tier of {{"of": "to_base:tag / to_stars:turbine"}}.

## Fan

When mounted to any other block, {{"of": "self"}} can instead be powered directly with {{"of": "to_base:concept / minecraft:redstone"}} to push entities and mobs.

The strength of the redstone signal controls how fast the blades spin, which in turn controls the strength and range of the airflow they generate.

At full signal strength, the airflow can push entities standing in front of the {{"of": "self"}} up to 8 blocks away.

The strength of the fan weakens depending on the distance an affected entity is from the blades.

Flying creative players are unaffected, and any entity behind a solid block is shielded from the draft. Sneaking greatly reduces the pushback for survival players, and stops it completely for creative players.

# Hazards

Standing too close to any spinning {{"of": "self"}} will deal damage (depending on the rotational speed) and knock the offending entity away.

# Cosmetic

{{"of": "self"}} are rotated by 45 degrees alternating with the height it is placed at.

When rendered as equipment on a mob, such as in the hand or as a helmet, the blades will spin at a speed proportional to the entity's velocity.

When moved around in an inventory screen by a player, a {{"of": "self"}} will spin at a speed proportional to the motion of the mouse, gradually slowing back down to a stop.
