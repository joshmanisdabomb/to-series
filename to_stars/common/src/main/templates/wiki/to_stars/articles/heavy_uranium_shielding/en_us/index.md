# Introduction
The {{"template": "introduction", "description": "a craftable block"}}. It has a high blast resistance against {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:nuclear_explosion", "plural": true}}, but a lower blast resistance against other types of explosions.

# Nuke Damage Prevention
{{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:nuclear_explosion", "plural": true}} will be stopped from breaking any more blocks behind each block of {{"of": "self"}}.

When stopping a nuclear blast, this block has a low chance of breaking - meaning subsequent explosions could damage blocks past the shielding.

## Entity Damage
Since entity damage is calculated after block damage in a {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:nuclear_explosion"}}, being behind a single layer of {{"of": "self"}} has a chance to kill entities behind it even though blocks will not be damaged.

# Tips
It's recommended to place multiple layers of shielding if you want to protect against a {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:nuclear_explosion"}}. You can roughly place layers equal to 1 + the amount of nuclear blasts you intend to defend against.
