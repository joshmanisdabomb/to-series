# Introduction
The {{"template": "introduction", "description": "a cable block"}}. It can be used to transfer {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_base:to_energy"}} between {{"of": "to_base:tag / to_stars:machine", "plural": true}}.

# Drawbacks

When an amount of energy is transferred through this cable, the total energy transmitted drops by 2%. This is compounded for each {{"of": "self"}} that energy transfers through to get from its source to its destination.

Also, whilst energy is transferred through this cable, there is a chance every tick that the electricity may arc to nearby mobs. The damage dealt is proportional to the amount of energy transferred.

## Upgrading

To remove both these issues, {{"of": "self", "plural": true}} can be crafted with {{"of": "minecraft:item / to_stars:heavy_duty_rubber"}} to remove the energy loss.
