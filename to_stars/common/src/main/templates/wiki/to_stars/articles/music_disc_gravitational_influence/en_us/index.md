# Introduction

{{"template": "introduction", "description": "a music disc"}}. It is rewarded to the first player on the server to detonate an {{"of": "minecraft:block / to_stars:atomic_bomb"}}, even in singleplayer.

It can be played in a {{"of": "minecraft:block / minecraft:jukebox"}} with a comparator output strength of 1.

# Music

The song is *Gravitational Influence*, a dark spacesynth track produced by {{"of": "to_base:author / to_stars:k3ylet"}} in 2021.

It has a total runtime of 2:29.35.

# Obtaining

Once the first {{"of": "minecraft:block / to_stars:atomic_bomb"}} is detonated and the {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:nuclear_explosion"}} occurs, the player will immediately receive the disc in their inventory.

If the awarded player is killed in that same {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_stars:nuclear_explosion"}}, the disc will be dropped amongst their items but could burn in {{"of": "minecraft:block / to_stars:nuclear_fire"}}.

Similar to a {{"of": "minecraft:block / minecraft:dragon_egg"}}, there can only be one disc per server (without duplication exploits).
