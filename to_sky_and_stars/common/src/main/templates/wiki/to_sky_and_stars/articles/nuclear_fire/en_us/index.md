# Creation

This block is placed by {{"of": "to_base:concept / to_sky_and_stars:nuclear_explosion", "plural": true}}, appearing more commonly at the edges of the blast.

# Spreading

This block behaves similarly to {{"of": "minecraft:block / minecraft:fire"}}, spreading to nearby blocks with solid faces. It can spread 2 blocks horizontally and 3 blocks vertically.

Once spread, the new block will have its age increased by 1-3. Once the age reaches 15, the fire will no longer spread and will dissipate on its own.

When spreading, blocks in the previously mentioned range will have a chance to be converted into {{"of": "minecraft:block / to_sky_and_stars:nuclear_waste"}}. These blocks must have a blast resistance lower than 100. Nearby air blocks also have a chance to be filled with waste, which will instantly fall to the ground.
